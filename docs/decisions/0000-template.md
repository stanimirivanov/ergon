# ADR NNNN: Short decision title

- Status: Proposed
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Deciders: names or roles

## Context

What problem forces a decision now? State constraints, relevant evidence, and
the consequences of doing nothing.

## Decision

State the decision and its boundaries precisely.

## Alternatives

List credible alternatives and why they were not selected.

## Consequences

Record benefits, costs, risks, compatibility effects, and follow-up work.

## Verification

How will the decision be demonstrated, monitored, or revisited?
